Where the work is today

The transaction layer for agents, agent sandboxes, GPU cloud tooling, and the people who build with all of it.

I spend my time where new infrastructure meets the developers who have to make it work: the resources agents pay for, sandboxes they run in, open-weight inference, and the builder communities that form around all of it.
